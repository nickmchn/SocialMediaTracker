Question Post
Every Monday
  • Opinion or experience-based — easy to answer
  • Slightly provocative or funny
  • Open-ended — pulls a story or take out of people
  • No yes/no questions
  • No personal financial info requests
  • No guilt trips or shame angles
  • Respond to every comment — that's the whole game
Story / Insight
Wed or Thu — 150–250 words max
  • Real client situation (anonymized) or personal observation
  • One sentence hook that stops the scroll
  • 3–4 short paragraphs
  • One takeaway or question at the end
  • SOF background earns its place — shared experience, not credentials
  • About them, not about how smart you are
Human Post
Friday — lightest lift
  • Real life: training, family, a book, an observation
  • No financial agenda
  • Reminds people there's a real person behind the profile
  • Often gets the most engagement
  • Keep it short — 2–4 sentences is fine
LinkedIn Article
Once per month — 500–800 words
  • Built around a question your prospect is actually Googling
  • Question-based title, answer in paragraph one
  • Subheadings throughout
  • AI citation / SEO play — lives on your profile permanently
  • AI drafts, you make it yours
Never Post
  • Market commentary or predictions
  • Anything that sounds like a product pitch
  • Generic financial tips anyone could write
  • The words "tax-efficient," "holistic planning," or "comprehensive"
  • Political opinions
  • Anything that fails the evergreen test: still relevant in 3 years?
The Weekly Habits
Non-negotiable
  • Before every post: spend 10–15 min commenting genuinely on 5 accounts in your target audience. Primes the algorithm.
  • Every Friday: look up every person you emailed or met with that week on LinkedIn. Send a connection request. This compounds fast.
  • The North Star: every post should make a high-income veteran think "he gets my situation" — not "he's impressive."
